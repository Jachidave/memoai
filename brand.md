## Logo Brief

**Idea:** A minimalist leaf sprouting from a document, symbolizing AI growth from meeting notes.

**Mark type:** Symbol plus wordmark

**Requirements:**
- Must work at 16px (favicon) and as 1024px app icon
- Deliverables: SVG, 1024x1024 app icon (no transparency for iOS), favicon set, social image 1200x630
- **Must not resemble the original's mark:** No shared blue color, no leaf/document shapes similar

**Typography:** Clean sans-serif (Inter or similar)

## Voice

**Tone words:** Clear, Helpful, Reliable

**Does not mean:** Corporate jargon, marketing fluff, technical

**Do/Don't pairs:**

| Says this | Not "this" |
|-----------|------------|
| "Your meeting, summarized" | "Enterprise-grade AI solutions" |
| "Works offline" | "Powered by cutting-edge neural networks" |
| "Save your notes" | "Optimize your knowledge management workflow" |
| "Quick preview" | "Advanced natural language processing" |
| "Drag to upload" | "Leverage synergistic methodologies" |
